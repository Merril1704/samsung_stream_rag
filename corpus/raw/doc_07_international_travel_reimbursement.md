title: International Travel Reimbursement Policy
---

## §1 Additional Documentation Requirements
International trips require all receipts to include the original currency amount alongside the INR-converted amount. A foreign currency receipt verification form must accompany the expense report; reports missing this form will be returned for correction.

## §2 Approval Requirements
International travel reimbursements require Senior Director approval regardless of amount, in addition to the standard manager approval required for domestic travel.

## §3 Currency Conversion Rules
Currency conversion uses the exchange rate published by Finance on the date of the transaction, not the date of reimbursement submission.
