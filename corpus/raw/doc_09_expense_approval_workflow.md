## §1 Standard Approval Chain
Expense reports under ₹25,000 require only direct manager approval.

## §2 Elevated Approval Thresholds
Expense reports involving international travel, late-booked travel (per Travel Booking Exception Policy §2), or amounts exceeding ₹25,000 require Senior Director approval in addition to the manager sign-off.
