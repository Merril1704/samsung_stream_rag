title: Travel Booking Exception Policy
---

## §1 Standard Booking Window
Travel bookings should be made at least 7 days in advance through the approved travel portal.

## §2 Post-Travel / Late Booking Exceptions
When a booking is made after travel has already occurred (e.g., emergency rebooking or after-the-fact expense submission), the employee must attach a written justification and obtain approval from both their manager and the Travel Exceptions desk before the expense will be processed.

## §3 Emergency Travel Provisions
Emergency travel arranged with less than 24 hours' notice is exempt from the standard booking window in §1 but still requires post-trip justification per §2.
