## §1 Standard Menu Packages
Three standard packages are available for booked events: Package 1 (breakfast/coffee service), Package 2 (working lunch), and Package 3 (full-day catering including breakfast, lunch, and afternoon refreshments).

## §2 Dietary Accommodations
Vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options are available on request with a minimum of 48 hours' notice to the catering vendor.

## §3 Venue-Specific Catering Restrictions
Venue B and Venue C accept external catering vendors without restriction beyond the standard notice periods in §2. Documentation of Venue A's dietary-accommodation and external-vendor policy is not available in this corpus version; confirm directly with venue management before booking.
