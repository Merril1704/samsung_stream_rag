## §1 Venue A — Riverside Business Center
Capacity: 40 seated / 60 standing. Includes projector, whiteboard, and standard AV. On-site catering limited to the venue's preferred vendor list. Booking lead time: 5 business days.

## §2 Venue B — Grand Hall Conference Suite
Capacity: 30 seated theatre-style. Offers breakout rooms for up to 3 concurrent sessions. External catering permitted with 48-hour advance notice to venue management.

## §3 Venue C — Tech Park Conference Center
Capacity: 100 seated. Includes tiered seating, live-stream equipment, and in-house AV support. External catering requires a venue liability waiver.
