---

## §1 Booking Procedure
All employees planning an offsite workshop, training session, or customer-facing event must submit a booking request through the Events Portal at least 10 business days before the event date. Requests must include expected attendee count, preferred city, and event duration.

## §2 Capacity & Approval Tiers
Events for up to 50 attendees may be approved by the requesting employee's direct manager. Events exceeding 50 attendees require sign-off from the Facilities Coordinator. Events exceeding 150 attendees require Regional Operations approval.

## §3 Standard Lead Times
Venue confirmation typically takes 3-5 business days after submission. Catering must be finalized no later than 5 business days before the event.
