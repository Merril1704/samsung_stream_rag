## §1 Vendor Payment Terms
Standard vendor payment terms are net-30 from invoice date unless otherwise negotiated.

## §2 Event Vendor Cancellation Clause
Event vendors contracted directly (catering, AV, staging) may enforce a 14-day cancellation notice period for full refund eligibility, which may differ from the internal Event Cancellation & Refund Policy's 30-day window; employees should confirm vendor-specific terms at time of contracting.
