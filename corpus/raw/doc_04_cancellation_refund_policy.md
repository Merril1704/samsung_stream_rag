---

## §1 Standard Cancellation Windows
Bookings cancelled more than 30 days before the event date receive a full refund of any deposit paid. Cancellations made 15-30 days before the event receive a 50% refund. Cancellations made fewer than 15 days before the event are non-refundable.

## §2 Refund Tiers
Refunds are processed within 10 business days of cancellation confirmation and issued to the original payment method or cost center.

## §3 Force Majeure Exceptions
Cancellations resulting from documented force majeure events (natural disaster, government-mandated closure) are exempt from the standard cancellation windows in §1 and are eligible for full refund regardless of notice period.

## §4 Non-Refundable Deposits
Certain premium venues (see Venue Directory) may designate a portion of the deposit as non-refundable at time of booking; this will be disclosed at booking confirmation.
