---

## §1 Eligible Expenses
Eligible reimbursable expenses for domestic business travel include airfare/train fare, lodging, ground transportation, and meals within per-diem limits.

## §2 Receipt & Documentation Requirements
All expenses over ₹500 require an itemized receipt submitted through the Expense Portal within 15 days of trip completion.

## §3 Reimbursement Timeline
Approved reimbursements are disbursed within 7 business days of final approval.
